Get the TigoMeNote app

Daily notes, photos, Explore, and Tigo. Free.

⬇ Download for Android Loading…

Downloaded? Tap the notification, or open Files → Downloads → TigoMeNote.apk.

  1. Tap Download. The file is TigoMeNote.apk.
  2. Open the file from the notification or your Downloads.
  3. If Android asks, allow "Install unknown apps" for Chrome (or your browser), then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open and sign in with your TigoMeNote account.

🔒 The app is signed by TigoMeNote. It's not on Google Play yet, so Android shows a warning; that's normal. To update, download again and install over it — your data stays.

There's no App Store app yet. On iPhone, add TigoMeNote to your Home Screen: it opens full screen like an app and can get notifications.

  1. Open tigomenote.com in Safari. Not inside Instagram, Messenger or another app.
  2. Tap Share ⬆︎ at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen", then Add.
  4. Open TigoMeNote from your Home Screen and sign in.
  5. Turn on notifications: Settings → Notifications → Turn on → Allow.

🔔 Notifications on iPhone need iOS 16.4 or newer, and only work when TigoMeNote is opened from the Home Screen icon (not from a Safari tab).

Scan with your phone camera to get the app.

  1. Open the camera on your phone.
  2. Point it at the QR code.
  3. Tap the link that appears, then follow the steps for Android or iPhone.

Or open tigomenote.com/get on your phone.
