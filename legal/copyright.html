Respecting your content and other people's work.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Your content

You own the photos, videos and text you post. You give TigoMeNote only the limited license described in the Terms of Use so we can show your content to the people you choose.

Other people's content

Only post content you created or have permission to share. Do not upload movies, music, photos or other work that belongs to someone else without permission.

Reporting copyright infringement

If you believe content on TigoMeNote infringes your copyright, send a notice using the Report button in the app (choose “Something else”). Include:

  1. Your name and how to contact you.
  2. A description of your work that was copied.
  3. The link to the post or account on TigoMeNote.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not allowed by you, your agent or the law.
  5. A statement that your notice is accurate and that you are the owner or allowed to act for the owner.
  6. Your full name as a signature.

We will remove or hide content that clearly infringes, and tell the person who posted it.

Counter-notice

If your content was removed by mistake, contact us with the link, an explanation, and your full name. We may restore it if the claim was wrong.

Repeat infringers

Accounts that repeatedly infringe copyright will be suspended.
