About TigoMeNote

A social app for your daily notes, photos and the people you care about, with safety built in.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

What TigoMeNote is

TigoMeNote lets you keep a private Daily, organise photos and videos in a Gallery on your own device, share posts and statuses with friends, and stay connected with your Family and Friends through Locate and SOS.

Our promises

  • Safety first. SOS works even during sleep time or when a daily limit is reached.
  • Consent. Nobody can add you to a circle or see your location unless you accept.
  • Your photos stay yours. Your Gallery stays on your device. Only what you choose to post is uploaded.
  • No ads, no selling of data.
  • Age 13+. Teens (13–17) get a private account and can be supervised by a parent, always visibly.
