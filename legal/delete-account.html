Delete your account

For TigoMeNote, TigoMeNote Chat and Tigo — one account for all three apps.

In the app

Open Settings → Account → Delete account, enter your password and type DELETE.

Here on the website

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, posts, photos and status, comments, likes, saves and shares.
  • Followers and following, Family & Friends, location history and SOS alerts.
  • Tigo (your pet, coins and house), reminders, notifications and sign-ins on all devices.
  • Your messages are blanked in other people’s chats, so their conversations stay readable.

Deletion happens right away and can’t be undone. Server backups are overwritten within 30 days.

What stays

Files on your own phone are yours and are not touched: photos in Pictures/TigoMeNote, your Daily backup in Documents/TigoMeNote, and Daily notes saved in the app on your device until you uninstall it.
