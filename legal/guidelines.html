Community Guidelines

What is not allowed on TigoMeNote, and how we keep everyone safe.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

TigoMeNote is for real people and their families. These rules apply to posts, statuses, comments, profiles, names and bios. Breaking them can lead to removal, strikes, or suspension.

Not allowed

Violence and threats

No threats to hurt anyone, no encouraging violence or attacks, no graphic gore, and no celebrating violent events or the people who committed them.

Nudity and sexual content

No nudity, pornography, sexual acts, or offers or requests for sexual content or services.

Child safety

Zero tolerance. No sexual content involving anyone under 18, no sexualising children, and no contacting minors for sexual purposes. Such content is removed, the account is suspended, and the matter may be reported to the authorities.

Illegal firearms and weapons

No buying, selling, trading or giving away of firearms, ammunition, parts or explosives; no homemade or unregistered guns; no instructions to make weapons or convert them. Showing weapons to threaten or intimidate is also not allowed.

Drugs and other crime

No selling or buying illegal drugs, no fake documents or money, no stolen goods, no fraud or scams, no hacking services, no human trafficking, and no planning or bragging about crimes.

Bullying, harassment and hate

No bullying, humiliating, or repeatedly contacting someone who doesn't want it. No attacks on people because of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or illness.

Suicide and self-harm

No encouraging suicide or self-harm, and no telling anyone to hurt themselves. If you or a friend is struggling, please reach out to someone you trust or local emergency services.

Privacy and safety

Do not share other people's private information (address, phone, location, ID) or photos of them without permission. Never use Locate to follow someone who didn't agree.

Spam and fake accounts

No spam, misleading links, fake giveaways, or pretending to be someone else.

How we enforce these rules

  1. Automatic filter. Captions, comments, names and bios are checked automatically. Clear violations (for example selling guns or drugs, threats, pornography) are blocked and cannot be posted. Borderline words are sent to an admin to review.
  2. Reports. Anyone can report a post, comment, status or account using Report. Reports are private.
  3. Hidden while reviewed. Content reported by 3 or more people, or reported for child safety, is hidden from others until an admin decides.
  4. Decisions. An admin may dismiss the report, hide or delete the content, give a strike, or suspend the account.

Photos and videos are reviewed through reports. Automatic image detection may be added later.

In an emergency

If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 (Bahrain) or 911 (Philippines) first, then use SOS or Report.

Think a decision was wrong?

Contact us using the Report button in the app (choose “Something else”) and explain what happened. We will take another look.

These policies are written in English. If a translation is shown elsewhere in the app, the English version applies.
