Where do your files go?

Daily (private)
On your device only. In the app: its own TigoMeNote folder. Not uploaded.
Share to Explore
Only a small copy is uploaded (photo ~1600 px, video ≤15 s). If you're offline, it waits on your device and uploads automatically when you're back online. You can schedule the time.
