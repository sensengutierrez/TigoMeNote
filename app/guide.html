How to use TigoMeNote

A quick tour, from Daily to Profile. Tap a topic.

Daily

Your day in one place.

  1. Write notes and to-dos, and pick a mood. Daily notes stay on your phone (backed up to Documents/TigoMeNote).
  2. See the weather for your location. Tap it for details: humidity, wind, pressure, UV, sunrise, the next 24 hours and 7 days.
  3. Tap the date at the top to open the calendar: colored dots for classes, work, reminders and notes, and each day's events.
  4. Tap Add under Reminders & alarms. Your phone gets a notification at the right time, even when the app is closed.
  5. Two clocks: tap ✎ to choose the time zones (for family abroad).
  6. Exchange rate: today's rate for your country's money (for example BHD → USD). Change the two currencies, type an amount, or tap ⇄ to swap.
  7. Sleep & breaks: set your sleep time, a break reminder and a daily limit. SOS and Family still work.

School & work

Your weekly class and work schedule.

  1. On Daily, tap 📚 Class or 💼 Work.
  2. Type the name and place, pick the days (or Weekdays, Mon·Wed·Fri…), then the start and end time.
  3. Choose Remind me (for example 15 min before). You get a notification before each class or shift.
  4. Tap a day on top to see that day. Now shows what is happening right now. Tap an item to edit or delete it.

Gallery

Photos saved on your phone.

  1. Tap Camera to take a photo or video, or Upload to add from your phone. They are saved in Pictures/TigoMeNote.
  2. Make albums and move photos into them. Your phone folders show below; tap ▾ to hide or show them.
  3. Press and hold a photo in a phone folder to set it as the folder cover. Tap ✎ to rename a folder (only inside TigoMeNote).
  4. The Uploading box shows posts that are still being sent.

Share a post

Words or photos — videos stay on your phone.

  1. Tap the big ＋ button.
  2. Take photo with filters, or From gallery to choose up to 10 photos (tap in the order you want).
  3. Write a caption, choose who can see it (Public, Followers, Only me) and tap Post. You can also schedule it.
  4. No internet? It waits on your phone and posts when you are back online.
  5. Just words? In Explore or your Profile, tap What's on your mind?, write, and tap Post. No photo needed (tap Add photos if you want one).

Explore

Posts and stories from people you follow.

  1. Swipe through the feed. Tap ❤️ to react, 💬 to comment, and ↻ to repost.
  2. Tap a circle on top to watch a status. Tap Your status to add one (photo).
  3. Switch between For you and Following. Tap a name to open their profile.
  4. Tap What's on your mind? to write a post right here. Tap 🖼 to add photos.

Safety & SOS

Family & Friends, location, and emergency SOS.

  1. Tap Add people and type their @username. They must accept before anyone sees a location.
  2. Turn location sharing on or off for each person, or pause it for everyone.
  3. Tap Locate to see your family and friends on the map.
  4. SOS: press and hold the red button for 3 seconds. After a 5-second countdown (you can cancel), your Family & Friends get an alarm with your location.
  5. In a real emergency, also tap Call 911.

Messages

Chats with family and friends.

  1. Tap ✎ to start a chat or a group. New people go to Requests first.
  2. Send text, photos, emoji reactions and your location.
  3. Stickers: open 😊 → Stickers. Tigo 3D stickers move, and Tigo talks when you send or tap one.
  4. Tap ⋯ in a chat to change the theme, wallpaper and bubbles.
  5. Add the Messages widget to your home screen (long-press home screen → Widgets → TigoMeNote).

Tigo

Your little pet friend.

  1. Feed, play and clean Tigo to keep the bars full. Food, fun and clean go down slowly; energy does not, and games don't make Tigo tired.
  2. On the island, walk near things and a button appears: 🪑 Sit on the bench, 🎣 Fish at the pond (tap Pull! when the float dips), 🌱 Garden (buy seeds, plant, and harvest for coins), 🍎 Pick fruit from the trees every 4 hours.
  3. Walk around the island: Tigo's house, garden, pond and path. The lights turn on at night.
  4. Play games to earn coins and XP, then dress Tigo up in the Closet.
  5. Tap Avatar 🧒 to play as a Kid instead of the note: pick a ready-made look, or choose skin, hair, top, bottom and shoes. Switch back to Tigo any time.
  6. Friends playing: open Games to see people you follow who are in Tigo now. If they have a room open, tap Join.
  7. 👥 Friends: find people and tap Follow to make them your Tigo friends. See who is online, tap Join to enter the game they are playing, or 💬 to chat.
  8. 🌍 World chat is for everyone in Tigo, and 🏝️ Here is only for the people on the island you're on. Words only, no links, be kind. Tap a message to Mute or Report someone.
  9. 💬 Chats are Tigo's own private chats and groups — they stay in the game (not in Messenger). You can chat with friends who follow you back; tap New group to make one.
  10. ✈️ Visit a friend: Tigo flies over the world to their island. Walk around, go inside their house, and see them move if they are online. You can look but not change their things. Tap 🏠 Home to fly back.
  11. In Lights Out, drag the screen to turn the view and pinch to zoom in or out.
  12. Put a 📺 TV in the house and tap 🪑 Sit near it — Tigo watches the show.
  13. Walk up to the 🪞 Mirror and tap Look to see yourself. Near the 🐠 Aquarium, tap Feed fish and watch them come for the food.
  14. Tap your 🐶 pet to choose: 🐾 Come (follows you), ✋ Stay (waits, and naps after a while) or 🌿 Free (walks around on its own). Also in ＋ Game menu.
  15. ⚽ Play puts a ball down that stays: walk into it to kick it, and your pet chases it too. Tap ⚽ Done playing when you're finished.
  16. At night Tigo sleeps in bed under a blanket; in the morning Tigo sits up and gets out of bed.
  17. 🌱 In the Garden, tap 💧 Water: watered plants grow 2× faster for 2 hours, and you'll see them get bigger.
  18. 🏡 House (in Decorate): upgrade to a Cottage, Villa and Mansion. Each one is bigger outside and has more room inside, and each costs more.
  19. 🚲 Rides (in Decorate): buy a bike, scooter or car. It parks outside — walk up and tap Ride to go faster, Get off to park.
  20. New furniture: the 🎮 Gaming chair.
  21. 💎 Gem Duel: 1 vs 1 match-3 on the same board. Swipe two gems next to each other to make 3 or more. Each turn has 2 moves; 4 in a row, an L or 5 in a row make special gems and give you an extra move. Fill 💥 Blast, tap it, then tap a gem to blow up a 3×3. After 5 rounds the higher score wins.
  22. ⚔️ Ranked duels win or lose 🏆 trophies: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond → Master. If nobody is free, you duel a ranked bot. See the best in Ranking (World or Friends). Every month is a new season. You can also play 🤖 Practice or 👥 Duel a friend with a room code (no trophies).
  23. 🛏️ Bedding (in Decorate): pick a blanket and pillow for the bed — try them on first, then buy.
  24. ✥ In Decorate, after you pick an item, use the ▲ ◀ ▶ ▼ arrows to move it bit by bit (hold to keep moving).
  25. 🚲 Rides: only one ride is parked outside — pick it in Decorate → Rides. Tigo holds the handlebar or wheel (rides are a little smaller now), pedals the bike and leans into turns.
  26. 🎨 Outside (in Decorate): change your house's colors — Classic, Mint, Bahay kubo, Sunset, Candy, Modern or Royal.
  27. 🎹 Put a Piano bench in front of the piano and tap 🪑 Sit — Tigo plays a song.
  28. 🛁 Bathroom (in Decorate): toilet, sink, shower, bathtub and jacuzzi. Walk up to the shower or tub and tap 🛁 Bath. Upgrade shower → bathtub → jacuzzi for a better bath (more joy and rest).
  29. 🍳 Kitchen (in Decorate): a counter and a stove. Walk up to the stove and tap 🍳 Cook. Upgrade Stove → Gas range → Chef kitchen for tastier, more filling food. An upgrade replaces the old one in the same spot.
  30. 🍽️ Sit on a chair at a table and tap Eat. Sit at the desk and Tigo plays on the computer.
  31. × In Decorate, tap the × on a thing in the list to put one away (you still own it). The arrow bar moves so it never covers the thing you picked.
  32. 🧱 Block avatar: a blocky toy buddy. In 🧒 Avatar, pick it and change the Body, Face, Clothing and hair — the preview turns, and you can drag it.
  33. 🐾 Pet beds (in Decorate → Furniture): a Dog bed, Cat basket, Bird nest or Pet cushion. Move them anywhere like other furniture. When Tigo sleeps — or your pet is on ✋ Stay for a while — your pet goes to its own bed and sleeps there.
  34. 🪜 A Villa or Mansion has stairs. Walk to them and tap Upstairs to decorate the 2nd floor and go out on the balcony.
  35. The island is bigger now, with a road around it — ride your bike, scooter or car all the way around.
  36. 💦 Water gun (Closet → Hand): tap Splash to spray. Your pet runs off, friends get bubbles, and it waters the garden.
  37. 🧒 New Kid looks: anime hair, sailor, gakuran, yukata and hero outfits, a pleated skirt and big ✨ anime eyes.
  38. Pets make sounds (woof, meow, tweet…), the computer on the desk shows games and music, and Tigo's mouth moves when Tigo talks.
  39. Bloom Clash 🌻 is a 3 vs 3 garden battle. Pick a hero: 🏹 Archer, 🛡️ Fighter, 🗡️ Assassin or 🔮 Mage. Your hero attacks by itself — you walk and tap ✨ Skill. Break the enemy Tower first, then their Great Flower. Play solo with bots, or Create a room for up to 6 friends (bots fill the empty spots).
  40. Lights Out 👻: find the keys and escape the dark school before the Hush catches you. Hide in lockers 🚪, flash it 🔦. Play solo, or tap Create a room and give the code to a friend. See the fastest escapes in Ranking.
  41. Sky Dash 🏃 race on a sky track (swipe only: left or right to switch lanes, up to jump; ride the slipstream), Gloom Siege 💦 bubble away waves of Gloomies together (drag the screen to look around; grab power-ups ⚡🔱💥🛡️❄️; every wave is a new place), and Sudoku 🧩 Easy, Medium or Hard. Each one: Create a room for up to 4 friends, or play solo.
  42. In the room: use the stick to walk and swipe to look around. Tap ✕ to exit.
  43. In Tigo's house, press and hold a chair, table or any item to edit it: drag to move, ⟳ rotate, 🗑 put away, ✓ done. One tap does nothing, so you won't move things by accident.
  44. The small buttons on the left are the quick ones (Feed, Play, Clean, Sleep, 💬 Chat). Tap ＋ at the top right for the Game menu with everything else and Tigo's size.
  45. New World chat messages show on the screen for a few seconds. Tap them (or 💬) to open the chat.

Profile

Your page.

  1. Tap Edit profile to change your photo, name and bio. Tap Cover for your background.
  2. My status shows the statuses you shared in Explore (for 24 hours) with how many people viewed them.
  3. Tap What's on your mind? to write a post, with or without a photo.
  4. Your posts are below (tap the Posts number to jump there). Filter by Photos or Scheduled. The ↻ tab has your reposts.
  5. My music: tap Add songs to pick songs, or a whole folder on your phone. They play in the app and in the music widget.
  6. The Family & Safety card has SOS and Locate, too.

Settings & privacy

Make it yours and stay in control.

  1. Appearance: dark mode, theme color, text size, UI style, and Home screen widgets (Solid, Glass or Clear).
  2. Background: a color, your photo, or a seasonal theme with sound.
  3. Privacy & supervision: private account, follow requests, and parent supervision for teens.
  4. Files on this device: back up and restore your Daily notes.
  5. Report a bug: something not working? Tell us what happened and add a screenshot. It helps us fix it fast.
  6. Delete account: permanently removes your account and what you posted.
