How to use TigoMeNote

A quick tour, from Daily to Profile. Tap a topic.

Daily

Your day in one place.

  1. Write notes and to-dos, and pick a mood. Daily notes stay on your phone (backed up to Documents/TigoMeNote).
  2. See the weather for your location. Tap it for details: humidity, wind, pressure, UV, sunrise, the next 24 hours and 7 days.
  3. Tap the date at the top to open the calendar: colored dots for classes, work, reminders and notes, and each day's events.
  4. Tap Add under Reminders & alarms. Your phone gets a notification at the right time, even when the app is closed.
  5. Two clocks: tap ✎ to choose the time zones (for family abroad).
  6. Exchange rate: today's rate for your country's money (for example BHD → USD). Change the two currencies, type an amount, or tap ⇄ to swap.
  7. Sleep & breaks: set your sleep time, a break reminder and a daily limit. SOS and Family still work.

School & work

Your weekly class and work schedule.

  1. On Daily, tap 📚 Class or 💼 Work.
  2. Type the name and place, pick the days (or Weekdays, Mon·Wed·Fri…), then the start and end time.
  3. Choose Remind me (for example 15 min before). You get a notification before each class or shift.
  4. Tap a day on top to see that day. Now shows what is happening right now. Tap an item to edit or delete it.

Gallery

Photos saved on your phone.

  1. Tap Camera to take a photo or video, or Upload to add from your phone. They are saved in Pictures/TigoMeNote.
  2. Make albums and move photos into them. Your phone folders show below; tap ▾ to hide or show them.
  3. Press and hold a photo in a phone folder to set it as the folder cover. Tap ✎ to rename a folder (only inside TigoMeNote).
  4. The Uploading box shows posts that are still being sent.

Share a post

Photos only — videos stay on your phone.

  1. Tap the big ＋ button.
  2. Take photo with filters, or From gallery to choose up to 10 photos (tap in the order you want).
  3. Write a caption, choose who can see it (Public, Followers, Only me) and tap Post. You can also schedule it.
  4. No internet? It waits on your phone and posts when you are back online.

Explore

Posts and stories from people you follow.

  1. Swipe through the feed. Tap ❤️ to react, 💬 to comment, and ↻ to repost.
  2. Tap a circle on top to watch a status. Tap Your status to add one (photo).
  3. Switch between For you and Following. Tap a name to open their profile.

Safety & SOS

Family & Friends, location, and emergency SOS.

  1. Tap Add people and type their @username. They must accept before anyone sees a location.
  2. Turn location sharing on or off for each person, or pause it for everyone.
  3. Tap Locate to see your family and friends on the map.
  4. SOS: press and hold the red button for 3 seconds. After a 5-second countdown (you can cancel), your Family & Friends get an alarm with your location.
  5. In a real emergency, also tap Call 911.

Messages

Chats with family and friends.

  1. Tap ✎ to start a chat or a group. New people go to Requests first.
  2. Send text, photos, emoji reactions and your location.
  3. Tap ⋯ in a chat to change the theme, wallpaper and bubbles.
  4. Add the Messages widget to your home screen (long-press home screen → Widgets → TigoMeNote).

Tigo

Your little pet friend.

  1. Feed, play and clean Tigo to fill the bars. They no longer go down by themselves, and games don't make Tigo tired.
  2. Walk around the island: Tigo's house, garden, pond and path. The lights turn on at night.
  3. Play games to earn coins and XP, then dress Tigo up in the Closet.
  4. Tap Avatar 🧒 to play as a Kid instead of the note: pick a ready-made look, or choose skin, hair, top, bottom and shoes. Switch back to Tigo any time.
  5. Friends playing: open Games to see people you follow who are in Tigo now. If they have a room open, tap Join.
  6. Lights Out 👻: find the keys and escape the dark school before the Hush catches you. Hide in lockers 🚪, flash it 🔦. Play solo, or tap Create a room and give the code to a friend. See the fastest escapes in Ranking.
  7. Sky Dash 🏃 race on a sky track (swipe left or right to switch lanes, swipe up to jump, ride the slipstream), Gloom Siege 💦 bubble away waves of Gloomies together (drag the screen to look around; grab power-ups ⚡🔱💥🛡️❄️; every wave is a new place), and Sudoku 🧩 Easy, Medium or Hard. Each one: Create a room for up to 4 friends, or play solo.
  8. In the room: use the stick to walk and swipe to look around. Tap ✕ to exit.

Profile

Your page.

  1. Tap Edit profile to change your photo, name and bio. Tap Cover for your background.
  2. My status shows the statuses you shared in Explore (for 24 hours) with how many people viewed them.
  3. Your posts are below (tap the Posts number to jump there). Filter by Photos or Scheduled. The ↻ tab has your reposts.
  4. My music: tap Add songs to pick songs, or a whole folder on your phone. They play in the app and in the music widget.
  5. The Family & Safety card has SOS and Locate, too.

Settings & privacy

Make it yours and stay in control.

  1. Appearance: dark mode, theme color, text size, UI style, and Home screen widgets (Solid, Glass or Clear).
  2. Background: a color, your photo, or a seasonal theme with sound.
  3. Privacy & supervision: private account, follow requests, and parent supervision for teens.
  4. Files on this device: back up and restore your Daily notes.
  5. Report a bug: something not working? Tell us what happened and add a screenshot. It helps us fix it fast.
  6. Delete account: permanently removes your account and what you posted.
